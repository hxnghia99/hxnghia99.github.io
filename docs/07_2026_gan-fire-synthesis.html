GAN-based Layout-Guided Fire Image & Mask Generation

First Author · 2023 – 2024 · Seoul, South Korea

Architecture

An end-to-end layout-conditioned GAN combining Image-to-Image and Layout-to-Image translation branches, generating photorealistic fire images from semantic layout maps while jointly producing pixel-aligned segmentation masks — removing the need for manual annotation.

Performance

Lowered FID by 4.79 versus the PaintByExample diffusion baseline. Synthesized data improved a YOLOv9 detector by +4.6% mAP and a customized U-Net segmentation model by +4.44% mIoU on a self-built fire dataset.

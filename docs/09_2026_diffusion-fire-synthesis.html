Diffusion-based Layout-Conditioned Multi-Spectral Fire Synthesis

First Author · 2024 – Present · Seoul, South Korea · Ongoing dissertation work

Architecture

A layout-conditioned diffusion model using a layout-aware cross-attention mechanism to steer generation from semantic layouts. Extended to synchronized cross-spectral synthesis by combining RGB and Near-Infrared (NIR) channels with background conditioning, producing paired RGB/NIR outputs from a single reverse diffusion process.
